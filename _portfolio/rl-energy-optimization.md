## Overview

HVAC systems are one of the largest energy consumers in buildings. In this project I studied whether reinforcement learning agents can learn temperature control policies that keep occupants comfortable while using less energy.

## What I did

- **MDP formulation.** Framed building HVAC control as a **Markov Decision Process** with defined state, action and reward spaces.
- **Simulation environment.** Built an **EnergyPlus** simulation environment from historical building operating data.
- **Agent comparison.** Trained and compared **PPO, DDPG and Q-learning** agents over **1,000+ simulation episodes**, so the controller adapts to changing indoor and outdoor conditions.
